---
name: post-trade-review
description: Review a completed trade against its original evidence, risk report and execution record.
---

1. Retrieve the trade case and linked intent, decision report, fills and journal evidence.
2. Compare planned entry, stop, size, costs and rationale with actual execution and outcome.
3. Distinguish process quality from whether the trade made money; do not infer a strategy edge from one case.
4. Propose a candidate lesson only when supported by evidence and a linked experiment.
5. Submit the review through the journal tool only after showing the draft and receiving permission.
